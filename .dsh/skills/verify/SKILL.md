# Verify

Identify claims that materially affect the decision. Check them against supplied artifacts or authoritative sources available in the current environment. For each finding, state evidence and whether it supports, contradicts, or cannot establish the claim. Separate facts from inference; never claim verification without evidence. Conclude with a verdict, material risks, and checks still needed. Do not modify the project unless asked.
